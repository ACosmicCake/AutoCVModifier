Pick jobs that have a tailored CV and press Auto apply. A Chrome window opens the employer's application page, uploads your CV and fills every field from your profile, CV and the job description. Forms are left open for you to check and submit unless "Submit automatically" is on. CAPTCHAs and sign-ins are always left to you.
